How to Use the Templates

Three steps from download to printed labels.

1

Download

Pick your size and download the Word template.

2

Customize

Open it in Microsoft Word (or Google Docs), then type room names, numbers or notes into each label.

3

Print

Print on matching label sheets. Run a test print on plain paper first and hold it up to your label sheet to check the alignment.

Tip: Pair your printed labels with a Legend Sheet at the front door so movers know which color goes to which room. Learn more on our Label Benefits page.
