Label the Sides, Not Just the Top

Labels on top disappear the moment boxes are stacked. Movers end up unstacking boxes just to find out where they go. Put labels on two sides, or all four sides plus the top, so the room color is visible from any angle.

Pro tip: For fragile boxes, add a Fragile label next to the room color.

How the System Works

Three steps from the first box to the last.

1

Assign a Color to Each Room

Pick a color for every room in the new home.

2

Label Every Box

Put the room's color on the sides of each box as you pack.

3

Tag the Doors

Place a Door ID label on each doorway and post a Legend Sheet at the entrance so movers know exactly where to go.

Door IDs & Legend Sheets

Cut Unload Time with Door IDs and Legend Sheets

Label the boxes by room, then match them at the new home. A Door ID on each room's doorway and a Legend Sheet at the front entrance show movers the color for every room. No questions, no guessing, no boxes left in the wrong place.
