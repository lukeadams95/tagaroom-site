The Best Borrower Gift Comes at Loan Approval, Not Closing

November 3, 2026

← All Posts
TL;DR: If you want your borrower to actually use your gift, give it at loan approval. By closing, most people are moving that day or that week, and they've already packed. A TAG-A-ROOM moving kit or label pack sent at approval shows up right when the packing starts, with your name or logo on it, and we can drop ship it straight to the borrower.

By the TAG-A-ROOM team. Color-coded moving labels, designed by real movers and made in the USA.

Most lenders think about a gift at closing. It's the big moment, everyone's at the table, and it feels like the natural time to say thanks.

Here's the problem. We run moving crews, and by closing day most borrowers are already boxed up. Some are moving that same day. A roll of moving labels handed over at the closing table is a nice thought, but the boxes are taped shut by then.

So most of our mortgage customers give their gift at loan approval instead. That's when the borrower knows the house is happening, and that's when the packing starts.

Why loan approval is the right moment

Think about where your borrower is when the approval comes through. They just got the news they were waiting for. The closing date is on the calendar. And now there's a whole house to pack.

That's the window where a moving kit does the most good:

  • It gets used for the whole move. Labels go on every box from the first one to the last one.
  • Your name is in front of them all week. Every time they grab the labels or the tape gun, they see who sent it.
  • It shows up when the stress does. Packing is the hard part. A gift that helps with the hard part gets remembered.

As our mortgage page puts it: borrowers remember the people who made their move easier.

Closing vs. loan approval: a quick comparison

Gift at closingGift at loan approval
Where the borrower isOften packed already, sometimes moving that dayJust approved, packing hasn't started
How much of the kit gets usedMaybe a few labels for the last boxesThe whole kit, start to finish
How long your name stays in front of themA day or twoThe whole move
What it says"Thanks for closing with us""We know what's next, and we've got you"

A closing gift isn't wrong. It just lands after the part of the move where a moving kit helps.

How the labels help your borrower

This is the part that makes it a gift and not just swag with a logo on it.

Each room gets its own color. Master Bed, Kitchen, Living Room, Storage and so on. The borrower puts the matching label on each box as they pack. Then they put a door ID tag on the door frame of the matching room in the new house.

On moving day, whoever is carrying boxes just matches the color on the box to the color on the door. The crew doesn't have to ask where anything goes, and the borrower doesn't end up with kitchen boxes in the garage.

The alert labels in the kits cover the rest: This Side Up, Do Not Stack, Open First, Fragile and Heavy.

White Labeled Moving Kit A also comes with a parts box label. Once the kit is unpacked, the gift box becomes a box for TV remotes, cables and other loose parts. It's a small thing, but it means your gift box is still sitting in their new house after the move.

Drop ship it straight to the borrower

You don't have to store boxes at the office or remember to bring one to a meeting. A lot of our mortgage customers have us drop ship the move kit right to the borrower's door.

That works well with loan approval timing. The approval comes through, you place the order, and the kit shows up at their current home while they're still packing. Ask about drop shipping when you order, or email info@tagaroom.com.

Make it part of your marketing

The lenders who get the most out of this don't treat it as a one-off. They make it a step in every loan. Approval comes through, a kit goes out. That's how TAG-A-ROOM ends up working like an extension of their marketing team.

A few ways teams set it up:

Brand every pack. Custom branded packs carry your logo and text, so every borrower gets the same look.

Co-brand with a realtor. The custom branded label packs are made to co-brand with realtors or another service provider. If you work closely with an agent, you can split the gift and both names go into the house.

Add a coupon. We can put discount coupons for moving services and supplies inside the package.

Order in bulk. If you're closing a lot of loans, you're not ordering one kit at a time. Bulk pricing is available, and you can contact us for custom or bulk orders.

FAQ

When should a lender give a borrower gift?

At loan approval. By closing, most borrowers are moving that day or that week and have already packed. A moving kit sent at approval gets used for the whole move.

What's a good loan approval gift?

Something the borrower uses while they pack. A branded moving kit or label pack goes on every box, so your name stays in front of them through the move.

Can I put my company logo on it?

Yes. The Custom Branded Home Label Pack and Custom Branded Move Organizer Pack carry your logo and custom text. Moving Kit A can include your logo and contact info on the flyer and thank-you label with orders of 5 or more.

Can you ship the gift directly to my borrower?

Yes. We offer drop shipping to your clients. Ask when you order or email info@tagaroom.com.

Is there a "Congratulations on your loan approval" option?

Yes. White Labeled Moving Kit A lets you choose from 3 sayings, and "Congratulations on your loan approval!" is one of them. Loan Approval Home Label Packs also come with a choice of 3 sayings on the front.

Are the labels removable?

The room labels in the home label packs and moving kits are permanent box labels. The Move Organizer Pack uses removable labels.

Do you do bulk orders for mortgage companies?

Yes. Bulk pricing is available, and you can contact us for custom or bulk orders.
