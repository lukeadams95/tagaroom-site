About TAG-A-ROOM®

Built by a Mover. Packed by Veterans. Trusted Nationwide.

The original color-coded moving and storage label system, created to answer moving's biggest question: “Where does it go?”

Our Story

It Started on a Moving Truck in San Antonio

More than 30 years ago, a San Antonio, Texas moving company owner wanted a better way to set his business apart. He created a color-coded label system that kept boxes and furniture organized, cut down on misplaced items and saved customers time and money. That simple idea became TAG-A-ROOM®.

From One Moving Company to Nationwide

What started with one moving company now serves customers across the country with hundreds of products.

Proudly Partnered with Veterans

TAG-A-ROOM® partners with the National Veterans Outreach Program (NVOP) and its Veterans Enterprises of Texas (VETS) initiative. Many of our products are Veteran Packed, providing employment and job training for disabled veterans in a supportive environment. It helps veterans who face barriers in the mainstream workforce rebuild their lives with dignity and purpose.
